Stash Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026. Applies to the Stash Mac app and the "Stash — Save to Mac" browser extension.

Summary

Stash keeps everything you save on your own Mac. We do not run a server that receives your data, we do not have accounts, and we do not use analytics, ads or tracking. We never sell or share your data.

What the extension handles

The extension acts only when you use it: when you click its button, press its shortcut, choose a right-click menu item, click the "Save to Stash" button on a post, bookmark a post on X, or start a bookmarks import. It then reads:

  • Website content you choose to save: the page address and title, selected text, images, and for posts on X the text, images, links and author.
  • A screenshot of the visible part of the tab, only when you ask for one.

The extension sends this content to the Stash app on your own Mac at http://127.0.0.1. It does not send it anywhere else. If the app is closed, the extension keeps the save in the browser's local extension storage and delivers it when the app starts.

The extension also stores, in local extension storage, the port of the Stash app and whether your X bookmarks were imported before. This data never leaves your browser.

The extension does not read pages you do not act on, does not record your browsing history, and does not collect passwords, payment details, messages or location.

What the Mac app stores

The app stores your saved items, images, notes and search index in ~/Library/Application Support/Snipper on your Mac. Search, text recognition in screenshots and the AI model run on your Mac. Your saved content is not uploaded for processing.

Network requests the app makes

  • It downloads the open EmbeddingGemma 2 search model from Hugging Face and the Transformers.js library from jsDelivr, once, and caches them on your Mac. These requests contain no saved content.
  • It downloads images and link previews for items you saved, directly from the websites they come from.

Those websites receive the normal information of any web request, such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

Your control

You can delete any item in the app. To remove everything, quit Stash and delete the folder above. Removing the extension deletes its local storage.

Children

Stash is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date.
